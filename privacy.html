Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The short version: Personally reads your spreadsheet inside your browser and never uploads it. Your emails go straight from your browser to Google or Microsoft to be delivered. We don't run servers, we don't use analytics or ads, and we don't sell or share your data.

This policy covers Personally for Gmail (a Chrome extension) and Personally for Outlook (an Outlook add-in), together "Personally". Personally is made by ACooperDev ("we", "us"). It lets you send a personalized email to each row of an Excel or CSV file from your own email account.

What Personally handles, and where it goes

Information What happens to it
Your spreadsheet Read inside your browser and kept in memory while the Personally panel is open. It is never uploaded. Personally for Gmail also saves the spreadsheet's column names in the extension's local storage so the Merge fields button in Gmail can list them. They're cleared the next time the panel opens.
The email you're writing Read from your open draft only when you click Use my Gmail message or Use this message, and used to build the personalized emails in your browser.
The personalized emails Sent from your browser to Google (Gmail API) or Microsoft (Microsoft Graph) for delivery. They are stored in your own Sent folder by your email provider, like any email you send. They don't pass through us.
Your email address and sign-in When you sign in, Google or Microsoft gives Personally a temporary access token and your email address. Personally uses the address to show which account you're sending from and to send tests to yourself. In Gmail, the token is kept in the browser's session storage and is erased when you close the browser. In Outlook, Microsoft's sign-in library keeps its tokens in your browser's storage for the add-in.

What Personally doesn't do

  • It doesn't read your inbox, contacts, calendar or past emails.
  • It doesn't upload your spreadsheet, and it doesn't keep copies of your emails.
  • It doesn't use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or cookies of its own.
  • It doesn't sell, rent or share your information with anyone except the services listed below that make it work.

Google user data

Personally for Gmail asks Google for two permissions:

  • gmail.send: to send the emails you create, from your account.
  • userinfo.email: to know your email address, so it can show which account is sending and send tests to you.

It doesn't ask for permission to read, change or delete your email. The information Personally receives from Google is used only to send the emails you ask it to send, is not stored beyond your browser session, and is not transferred to anyone.

Personally's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft user data

Personally for Outlook asks Microsoft for Mail.Send, to send the emails you create from your mailbox, and User.Read, to sign you in and read your basic profile. It doesn't ask for permission to read your mail. Microsoft data is used only to send the emails you ask it to send.

Services Personally relies on

  • Google (sign-in and Gmail API): privacy policy
  • Microsoft (sign-in and Microsoft Graph): privacy statement
  • PayPal (handles donations, only if you choose to donate through the link on this website; Personally never sends PayPal anything): privacy statement
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages hosts this website and the Outlook add-in's files; GitHub Releases hosts the download files and counts how many times they're downloaded; the download counts on this website's home page are read from GitHub when the page loads): privacy statement

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Removing the Chrome extension deletes everything it stored in your browser. The Outlook add-in stores nothing in your mailbox; clearing your browser's data for it removes its sign-in tokens.
  • To stop Personally from sending as you, remove its access in your Google account or Microsoft account (work and school accounts: My Apps).

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Personally never sees your password: you sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own pages, and the access they grant is limited to sending mail.

Children

Personally isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll say so on this page before it takes effect.
