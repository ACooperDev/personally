How it works

It works inside the Gmail or Outlook you already use, so there's nothing new to learn.

1

Write your email

Compose as usual. Click a column name to drop in a field like {{Company}}. For blank cells, add a default: {{First Name|there}}.

2

Pick your spreadsheet

Choose an .xlsx, .xls or .csv file. Personally finds the email column and skips rows without a valid address.

3

Preview, test, send

Flip through each personalized email, send yourself a test, then send them all. Download a results sheet showing what went out.

Download and install

Personally isn't in the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft's add-in store yet, so for now you install it yourself. It takes about two minutes.

Personally for Outlook

For Outlook on the web, the new Outlook for Windows, and Outlook.com accounts.

Download for Outlook
  1. Click Download for Outlook above. It saves a small file called personally-for-outlook.xml.
  2. Sign in to Outlook, then open aka.ms/olksideload. The Add-ins for Outlook window opens.
  3. Click My add-ins on the left and scroll to the bottom. Under Custom Addins, click Add a custom add-in, then Add from File…
  4. Choose the file you downloaded and click Install when asked.
  5. Start a new email. Open Personally from the ribbon, or from the Apps button if you don't see it there.
  6. Click Sign in with Microsoft in the Personally pane and approve sending mail as you.

Updates install themselves. With a work or school account, your IT admin may need to approve Personally first. If sign-in says it needs admin approval, send them this page.

Personally for Gmail

For Gmail in Google Chrome (or Edge, Brave and other Chrome-based browsers) on a computer.

Download for Gmail
  1. Click Download for Gmail above and unzip personally-for-gmail.zip. Move the personally-for-gmail folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents. Chrome runs it from there, so don't delete it.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions (type it into the address bar).
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the personally-for-gmail folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Personally. Then open or reload Gmail.
  6. Click the Personally icon to open its side panel, then Sign in with Google and allow it to send email as you.

While Google reviews Personally, sign-in shows a "Google hasn't verified this app" screen. Click Advanced, then Go to Personally. It can still only send mail, never read it.

To update, download the new zip, replace the folder's contents, and click the reload arrow on Personally's card in chrome://extensions.

Built to stay out of your business

Personally is small on purpose. It sends your email and doesn't collect anything along the way.

Your spreadsheet stays on your computer

Your file is read inside your browser and never uploaded anywhere. We don't run servers that could store it.

Sent from you, not a mailing service

Each email goes out from your own account, lands in your Sent folder, and replies come straight back to you.

Permission to send, nothing more

Personally asks only for permission to send mail. It can't read your inbox, contacts or past emails.

Questions

Is my spreadsheet uploaded anywhere?

No. Personally reads the file inside your browser. Only the finished emails leave your computer, and they go straight to Google or Microsoft to be delivered, the same as any email you send.

What can Personally see in my account?

In Gmail it asks for permission to send email and to know your email address. It can't read your inbox. In Outlook it asks to send mail as you and to read your basic profile. It reads the message you're writing only when you ask it to use it as the template.

How many emails can I send?

Personally has no limit of its own, but your email provider does: roughly 500 a day for personal Gmail, 2,000 for Google Workspace, and about 300 a day for Outlook.com. Work and school Microsoft 365 accounts allow up to 30 a minute. Personally paces itself to stay under these.

Will my emails end up in spam?

They're sent from your own account like any email you write, so they don't carry a mailing service's reputation. Send to people who expect to hear from you, and send yourself a test first to see how the message looks.

Can I include attachments or pictures?

Not yet. Links and formatting like bold and lists come through. For images, link to them instead of pasting them into the message.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. There's no trial, no paid tier and no ads, and nothing about you is collected or sold. Personally runs entirely in your browser, so it costs almost nothing to keep running. Donations help pay for the developer's time.

Can I use it at work?

Yes. Personally is free for personal and commercial use alike.
